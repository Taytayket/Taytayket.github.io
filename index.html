Questions

Funny shorts are ready within 2 business days after we receive a suitable photo. Story films are ready within 7 business days after you place your order.

For a funny short, upload 1 clear photo that shows your pet's face and body. Moon Stage Duo needs 1 photo of each pet. For a story film, our story guide helps you share your pet's story. Then you upload 3 to 5 clear photos of your pet, plus any photos or videos of the moments you'd like included, up to 20 files in total.

A real person on our team makes every video. Our AI story guide only helps you tell us your pet's story. It doesn't write or make the film.

Funny shorts don't include changes after you order. Each story film includes 1 free revision after delivery.

Payments are handled on Stripe's secure checkout page. Prices are in US dollars, and any applicable taxes are shown at checkout.
